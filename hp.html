About Us

The Comtrack has partnered with Sena Financial Services, RMA Life & Gen-Life to bring funeral and grocery cover to its customers for a low monthly fee*.
The grocery plan includes the mian member, spouse, six childern under the age of 21.

Comtrack now helps you breathe a sigh of relief.
You can now sit back and mourn your loved ones without the ups and downs of funeral arrangements.
As a member of our scheme, you obtain the privilege of dignified funeral service and having groceries delivered at your doorstep.

The value of a funeral and grocery cover are determined by the premium and the age of the deceased person.
Those over the age of 14 will receive groceries that cater to the capacity of 250 people, while those under the age of 14 will receive groceries that cater to the capacity of 100 people or less based on the plan type**.




*six (6) months waiting period for natural and accidental death and twelve (12) months for suicidal deaths is applicable.
**Capacity is based on the average number of people in attendance at funerals for each age group.
